TerraPulse — Smart IoT Environmental Telemetry

An end-to-end intelligent environmental telemetry system designed for real-time climatic and air quality monitoring. Driven by an ESP32 microcontroller, TerraPulse captures multi-sensor telemetry—ambient temperature, humidity, air quality index (CO2/smoke/NH3 via MQ-135), barometric pressure (BMP180), and precipitation states—and broadcasts the live stream to the cloud for real-time web telemetry.
